# Email writing

Follow the [shared product-email writing rules](https://github.com/GoldenBerry-SO/zurueckbleiben/blob/main/docs/writing.md#product-emails).

Lead with the reason for the email and the action the recipient needs. Keep codes and keys out of subjects
and inbox previews. Check expiry, renewal and account-state claims against the implementation. Use the
customer's product name in shared templates; do not add the delivery service's slogan. Welcome templates
must not claim that no account was created. Read rendered HTML and plain text with optional fields present
and absent. Review the caller's subject too. Do not send customer emails as part of a copy review.
